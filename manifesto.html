The agenda

THE SURGE: the full plan.

Grow. Learn. Thrive. Every student in this faculty deserves to grow beyond their degree, learn without being left behind, and thrive academically, professionally and personally.

My vision for this office is rooted in both experience and responsibility. Having served under the Office of the Vice President, I have gained hands-on experience in leadership, project management, teamwork and event coordination — firsthand insight into the responsibilities of the office and the opportunities it presents.

What follows is not merely a list of promises, but a structured plan informed by experience, guided by constitutional responsibility and driven by a commitment to the continued growth and development of our Faculty.

PILLAR ONE — GROW

The FASSA Empowerment Programme and The FASSA Market.

1FASSA Empowerment Programme (FEP)
•

Equipping FASSAITES with practical, marketable skills that complement their academic qualifications and open pathways to employment, entrepreneurship and income generation. Proposed to feature multiple skill areas, with two sessions dedicated to each, through interactive masterclasses led by experienced professionals — covering graphic design, marketing, photography, videography, content creation, content writing, ghostwriting and more. Built on existing commitments from facilitators and sponsors, with further partnerships to be pursued for implementation and sustainability.

2The FASSA Market
•

A proposed semesterly initiative giving FASSAITES with businesses and services a platform to showcase, promote and sell within the Faculty. Beyond creating opportunities for student entrepreneurs, it brings music, entertainment and a stage for emerging talent. External brands are engaged through strategic partnerships, with student vendors kept at the heart of the initiative.

PILLAR TWO — LEARN

The Vault and Tutorials.

3The Vault
•

A centralised digital archive making academic resources more accessible — past questions, lecture summaries, lab manuals and recommended learning resources, organised by department, level and course. Built in collaboration with departmental academic coordinators and course representatives, hosted on Google Drive, with a proper handover process to maintain it beyond this tenure.

4Tutorials
•

Academic challenges don't end at 100 level. Building on the existing tutorial structure to extend support to higher levels, working with departmental Vice Presidents and academic coordinators to identify the courses that present the biggest challenges at each level and close the gaps — making academic support more inclusive and responsive across the Faculty.

PILLAR THREE — THRIVE

The Social Experience, Student Welfare and Sponsorship & Partnerships.

5The Social Experience: FASSA Week
•

Building on the success of previous editions to make FASSA Week even more engaging and inclusive. Student feedback and opinion surveys shape what's planned, so FASSAITES have a real voice in the activities, experiences and memories the week is built around.

6Student Welfare
•

Serving as a bridge between FASSAITES and the Executive Council, so concerns are heard and receive appropriate attention. Working toward examination support packages and other relief initiatives where the need arises, in collaboration with relevant committees and departmental representatives — a culture of care, accessibility and responsive representation.

7Sponsorship and Partnerships
•

Pursuing collaborations with brands, organisations and businesses whose interests align with FASSA's goals, and strengthening FASSA's relationship with its alumni for continued engagement and support. The goal: lasting, mutually beneficial relationships that future executives can sustain beyond a single tenure.

FASSA deserves leadership that listens, takes responsibility and turns ideas into meaningful action.
Through GROW, LEARN and THRIVE, I envision a Faculty where students can develop practical skills, access academic resources and enjoy a more supportive and engaging Faculty experience.

Together, we SURGE FORWARD!
