The agenda

THE SURGE: the full plan.

Grow. Learn. Thrive. Every student in this faculty deserves to grow beyond their degree, learn without being left behind, and thrive academically, professionally and personally.

I am not running to keep things the way they are, nor am I approaching this role from the outside. I have served under the Office of the Vice President, and that experience shapes this plan.

What follows is not a list of promises — it is a plan I intend to achieve.

PILLAR ONE — GROW

Skill Academy and the Trade Fair.

1Skill Academy
•

Practical, portfolio-ready skills that complement the degree. Held once every three weeks across both semesters, each session is run by a confirmed, experienced professional, covering public speaking, content writing, graphic design, videography, entrepreneurship, marketing and more — each one built around something participants can add to their portfolio.

2Trade Fair
•

A termly Trade Fair, held up to three times a semester, giving FASSAITES with goods or services a platform to advertise, promote and sell within the faculty. Vendor slots are open to everyone, vetted for quality and safety, and completely free of charge.

PILLAR TWO — LEARN

The Vault and expanded Tutorials.

3The Vault
•

A structured, maintained digital archive of past questions, lecture summaries, lab manuals and recommended video resources, organised by department and level and accessible to every student instantly via Google Drive. Kept current in collaboration with departmental academic coordinators and course reps, and formally handed over to the incoming executive team beyond the 2026/2027 tenure.

4Tutorials
•

Working with the Faculty and departmental academic coordinators to expand tutorial reach, identify the courses most in need of support, and gather direct student feedback on timing, pacing, coverage and accessibility — so delivery keeps improving through the session.

PILLAR THREE — THRIVE

Social Experience, Welfare and Sponsorship.

5Social Experience
•

FASSA Week should reflect what students actually want. Input is gathered directly from students before planning begins, through surveys and departmental reps, and feedback is collected afterward to check the execution met expectations.

6Welfare
•

Relief packages and food banks to reach as many FASSAITES as possible. A personal point of contact for students across all departments, a welfare committee that puts student welfare first day-to-day, and direct advocacy within the Executive Council for every concern raised.

7Sponsorship
•

Reaching out to brands whose values and audience genuinely fit FASSA's activities, with well-structured proposals pitched early enough to commit ahead of major events. Alongside this, a lasting alumni engagement channel, so support doesn't depend on a single tenure to keep running.
