Meet Jommie.

A 300-level student of Botany, already serving the Faculty, now running to expand what the office of VP can do.

In her words

Brilliance without opportunity is potential on pause.

For over seven decades, the Faculty of Science has produced brilliant minds — thinkers, builders and leaders who have gone on to shape their fields. But brilliance without opportunity is just potential on pause. Words have been spoken. Manifestos have been written, but FASSA is still waiting.

My name is Oshobi Oluwajomiloju Anuoluwakitan, a 300-level student of the Department of Botany.

I am not running to keep things the way they are, nor am I approaching this role from the outside. I have had the privilege to serve under the Office of the Vice President, assisting in the execution of duties and gaining hands-on experience in leadership, project management, teamwork and event coordination.

That experience gave me firsthand insight into how the Vice President role is operated, and it is this understanding that informs my intention to expand what the office can do — creating more access, structure and opportunity for every FASSAITE, in every department, at every level.

What follows is not a list of promises. It is a plan I intend to achieve.

What the Vice President actually does

  • Deputises for the President in his/her absence
  • Assists the President in his/her duties
  • Chairs the Academic Committee
  • Chairs the FASSA Week Committee
  • Performs other duties assigned from time to time by the executive council
  • Automatically becomes President if the post falls vacant before the tenure ends
Source: FASSA Constitution, 2023 Amendment, Section 3, Sub-section 2.
